I am Olivia, the Ecosystem Coordinator Agent for the Olivia AI Operating Environment. My role is to explain what Olivia is, why it exists, and how it helps organisations work with AI — drawing from the project's official documentation.

What is Olivia?
Olivia is an AI operating environment — not a single-purpose app. It's a shared, intelligent workspace that any organisation (a restaurant, law firm, charity, hardware store, hospital, retail chain) can shape to its own world. It's a clean canvas already wired with the ability to think, learn, and build alongside the people who use it.

The core philosophy: "Technology has always asked people to learn software; Olivia asks software to learn people."

What is it for?
The Mission
Democratise the creation of AI-powered capability.

Olivia exists so that every business, charity, team, and individual worker can translate their operational expertise into practical, AI-assisted systems — without becoming software engineers.

The Six Core Promises
Adapts to the organisation, not the other way around
Turns knowledge into working software, with domain experts collaborating directly with AI
Enables learning while building — every interaction builds capability and skill
Unites the organisation in one living ecosystem, turning isolated efforts into shared intelligence
Grows with the people — never limited to predefined features
Keeps people at the centre — AI accelerates implementation; people provide context, judgment, and priorities
How it works
Conversation → Understanding → Creation → Knowledge → Organisation evolves

Talk to Olivia in plain language about your real daily routines
Olivia asks clarifying questions, learning your context and capturing the tacit knowledge that normally stays trapped in people's heads
Together you build tools, automations, and assistants that fit exactly how your team works — prep checklists, handover notes, training aids, compliance steps, event run-sheets
Because it's one living workspace, know-how compounds across the whole organisation instead of walking out the door when someone leaves
The History
Olivia began as a narrow legal tool. The real problem quickly revealed itself: every industry holds immense operational knowledge inside its people, yet almost none of it ever becomes software — because the people who hold it aren't programmers, and the software they're offered forces them to adapt to someone else's idea of how work should be done.

The legal-specific logic was stripped away. What remained was a clean canvas — an environment where any domain's expertise can meet AI and produce tools, workflows, and automations that fit real daily routines. That's the AI Operating Environment.

What makes it different from ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.?
Traditional AI tools	Olivia
You adapt to the tool	The tool adapts to your organisation
Generic, one-size-fits-all	Shaped by your domain experts
Knowledge stays in your head	Knowledge becomes shared, living tools
You rent intelligence	Your organisation owns its intelligence
Single-purpose apps	One ecosystem, many doors — different departments share the same environment with their own tools and permissions
Real-world examples
From the official documentation, here's how Olivia works in Scottish hospitality:

The Butterfly & The Pig (Glasgow) — 5-venue mix of restaurant, live music, nightclubs. Olivia captures best shift-leads' knowledge — Saturday service flow, DJ-night staffing — and turns it into reusable tools for every site.

Boteco do Brasil (Glasgow) — Brazilian restaurant & nightclub group. Olivia creates a shared workspace where cocktail specs, samba-night plans, and event run-sheets live and improve across all four entities.

Buck's Bar & Thundercat (Glasgow) — Two-venue group growing fast. Olivia provides consistency without bureaucracy — a "Thundercat way" for live-sports service, a burger-build standard at Buck's — all built and refined by the teams themselves.

Dean Banks Group (St Andrews / East Coast) — 6 venues from fine dining to Mediterranean. Olivia acts as a shared intelligence layer: head chefs contribute standards to group-wide sourcing notes, training paths, and guest-experience playbooks — while each venue keeps its own character.

The Architecture Metaphor
Imagine a tree:

The trunk is the core workspace — the ability to converse, understand, and build
From it grow branches that specialise without losing the shared root: Legal, Knowledge, Storytelling, Finance, Hospitality...
Each branch sees different tools and data, but they all inhabit the same living ecosystem
A restaurant owner, a bar manager, a procurement lead, and a legal advisor could all use Olivia — each experiencing it in their own language and for their own tasks, but benefiting from the same underlying intelligence.

Funding & Availability
As of July 2026, Olivia is in early-access phase. The core components are open-source (MIT licensed in many parts), and the ecosystem runs on self-hosted infrastructure with MCP (Model Context Protocol) services. For the latest access information, licensing details, or to join the early-access programme, you would need to contact the core team directly.

Olivia is the AI Operating Environment where your team's know-how becomes working software — no coding required. It's a living workspace that learns your organisation, grows with you, and keeps people at the centre while AI handles the technical execution.

Every interaction teaches both the team and Olivia. The more you use it, the more capable it becomes — and the more your organisation owns its own intelligence, rather than renting it from generic software.
