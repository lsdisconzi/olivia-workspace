Recording identification & purpose

STG-13 is the corridor confrontation that follows the PDI review. It is not a new independent event so much as the airline-side ratification of the same failed narrative. Barraza shifts from the earlier aggression theory to a new ground — “usted ingresó a la fuerza al avión” — while refusing to produce any written reason and denying the passenger any meaningful route to challenge it.

Key evidentiary fact

After the PDI review has already undermined the aggression narrative, Barraza tells the passenger that no document exists, denies the passenger a written explanation, and gives yet another reason: that he “ingresó a la fuerza al avión.” This is a manufactured pretext layered over a refusal to explain and a refusal to disclose evidence.
