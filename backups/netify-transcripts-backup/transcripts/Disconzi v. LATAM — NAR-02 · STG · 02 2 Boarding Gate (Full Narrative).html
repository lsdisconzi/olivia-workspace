Summary

The passenger interacts with LATAM gate staff about the boarding time display. The gate staff initially defends the boarding procedure, citing a 40-minute-before-departure policy. The passenger identifies a discrepancy between the displayed boarding time and the claimed policy. After repeated challenge, the gate staff concedes: **"ah no po, no ta correcto"** — the boarding status display was incorrect. The passenger also identifies that the gate was changed and flight times were being manipulated. This interaction provides the factual foundation for the passenger's later question to the pilot about the boarding delay — the question that triggered the [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]]'s retaliation and false accusation in STG-5.

Legal Significance

1. **Boarding display was incorrect** — LATAM's own gate staff concedes the boarding information provided to passengers was wrong. This is the factual predicate for the passenger's delay question to the pilot.

2. **Gate changed, times manipulated** — The passenger identifies both a gate change and schedule manipulation. This establishes that LATAM's boarding process at SCL on July 5, 2024 was procedurally irregular.

3. **"Engaño" (deception) claim** — The passenger states the boarding display "es como un engaño" (is like a deception). This captures the passenger's contemporaneous perception that LATAM was manipulating passenger information.

4. **Foundation for retaliation** — The passenger's legitimate questions about boarding procedures, based on admissions of incorrect displays, are the trigger for the [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]]'s retaliation in STG-5. The chain of causation: incorrect display → legitimate question → false accusation → forcible removal.

5. **Good faith established** — This recording establishes the passenger's good faith in asking about boarding times. He identified a genuine error (admitted by staff), challenged it politely, and was met with the staff's eventual concession.
