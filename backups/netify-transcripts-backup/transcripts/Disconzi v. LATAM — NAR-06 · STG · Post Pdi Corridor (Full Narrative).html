Transcript Analysis — STG-13 Post-PDI Corridor

**Transcript ID:** `I-002_07_NAR-06_STG_13_post_PDI_corridor`

**Date:** 2024-07-05

**Location:** Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (SCL), corridor immediately after the PDI office

**Language:** Spanish (Chile)

---

Summary

This short transcript captures the airline-side reaction to the passenger’s demand for a lawful explanation after the earlier claims have already failed. Instead of providing documentation or evidence, Barraza introduces a new explanation, invokes the camera, denies the document, and shifts the burden back to the passenger.

The legal value of this recording is not that it creates a new clean factual story. It is that it exposes the same manufactured removal logic as a moving target. It preserves the pattern of instability: aggression, then non-compliance, then forced entry, then denial of evidence and denial of reason.

    Legal reading

    The transcript shows a process failure in plain form:

    1. The passenger asks for the reason in writing.

    2. The airline responds by changing the reason.

    3. The airline invokes the camera, then refuses to show it.

    4. The airline denies that any written reason exists.

    5. The airline shifts the burden to the passenger to prove the negative.

    The result is a pattern of procedural evasion: the decision is preserved, but the legal basis is never made intelligible. The document never appears; the footage never appears; the burden never shifts back to the airline.

  • This stage: the airline adjusts the explanation rather than accepting the prior factual collapse.
  • The corridor confrontation therefore functions as the transition from accusation to denial-of-documentation and post-hoc rationalization.

    Conclusion

    STG-13 is a compact but decisive transcript. It confirms that the removal decision survives only through shifting grounds, withheld evidence, and refusal to provide the written explanation. This makes it a key part of the chronology showing persistence, asymmetry, and lack of lawful basis.
