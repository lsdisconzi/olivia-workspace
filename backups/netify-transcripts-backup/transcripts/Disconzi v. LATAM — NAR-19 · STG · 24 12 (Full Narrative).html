Legal Significance

1. **Independent passenger witness**: A lady seated next to the passenger "no vio nada" (saw nothing). This corroborates the PDI CCTV finding of no aggression. She is an identifiable witness — she was on the flight and seated adjacent.

2. **Passengers collectively questioning**: "todos estaban cuestionando" — the entire group of adjacent passengers was questioning the narratives. Multiple potential witnesses exist.

3. **Recorded admissions from authorities**: The passenger states he has audio recordings where LATAM security and DGAC discuss what the camera showed. These are the STG-6 and STG-7 recordings — already in evidence.

4. **Narrative shift identified in real time**: The passenger challenges LATAM directly: why did the narrative change? Because one story didn't work? This contemporaneous observation strengthens the case that the narrative shift was intentional and admitted.

5. **Dolo evidence**: The passenger asserts LATAM acted "de propósito" (on purpose) and "sabiendo que estaban mentiendo" (knowing they were lying). This is evidence of specific intent.

6. **Ban scope**: 85% of flights to the passenger's destination are operated by LATAM. The lifetime ban is effectively a regional travel prohibition.
