Why This Recording Matters

This is where the airline's removal narrative visibly mutates on tape — from "agressivo y" (STG_5) to "golpeó mi compañero", to "took decisions that don't correspond" (self-scan), to "ingresó igual" (forced entry) — while the very LATAM security officer dispatched to enforce the removal admits "no hai nada sobre pegarle a alguien". Multiple uninvolved passengers independently corroborate the version-shifting pattern. DGAC arrives, declines to verify any version against the camera, defers entirely to "lo que la compañía informa", and ultimately threatens physical force ("yo te voy a empujar"). This converts STG_5 from "wrongful removal" into a documented sequence of escalating false accusations under colour of state authority.

End-State Synthesis

The chain in one paragraph.

By the time STG_6 ends, the record contains: 1. Four mutually inconsistent removal grounds voiced by three different actors (LATAM stewardess, LATAM security Barraza, DGAC) within ~33 minutes: (i) "agressivo y" / aggression; (ii) "golpeó mi compañero" / hit a colleague; (iii) "tomó decisiones que no correspondem" / self-scanned own boarding pass; (iv) "no le autorizaron el embarque y usted ingresó igual" / unauthorised entry. 2. An on-tape concession by the LATAM security supervisor that "no mencionado nada sobre pegarle a alguien", demolishing ground (i)/(ii) at source. 3. Independent passenger testimony to the version-shifting and to the claimant's non-aggressive bearing throughout. 4. An on-tape state-side coercion ("yo te voy a empujar") carried out after the original ground has already been conceded unsupported. 5. An on-tape state-side verification abdication ("los motivos los dio la compañía y es lo más importante") that meets the threshold of PATTERN-OF-FAILURE at the state-actor limb. 6. PDI's on-tape acknowledgement of incomplete briefing ("ha me nunca mencionado eso") and that they "estaban tratando de identificar qué pasó".
