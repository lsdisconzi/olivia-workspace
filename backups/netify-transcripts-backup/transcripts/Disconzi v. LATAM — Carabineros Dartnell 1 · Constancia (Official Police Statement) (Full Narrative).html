Summary

On the morning after the SCL incident, [[SPK-passenger-leandro]] attends the Carabineros Dartnell sub-station near the airport to file a constancia (official police statement). He reports the full sequence: forcible removal from flight LA8159, PDI CCTV review confirming no aggression occurred, all three authorities (LATAM, PDI, DGAC) refusing to provide the carta de desembarque, the eventual receipt of an unsigned document containing PDI-disproven allegations, and LATAM's imposition of a lifetime travel ban. He reports having contacted his embassy. The constancia creates the first official law enforcement record of the incident.

Legal Significance

This recording establishes:

1. **First official complaint**: The constancia at Dartnell sub-station is the passenger's first formal law enforcement report of the incident, made approximately 16 hours after removal from the flight.

2. **Three-authority concealment**: The passenger specifically identifies that LATAM, PDI, and DGAC are all withholding the written explanation for removal — constituting a coordinated institutional barrier.

3. **The unsigned document**: The passenger documents receiving an unsigned paper containing reasons PDI had already "desprobadas" (disproven). This is direct first-person testimony about the falsified carta de desembarque, recorded within hours of receiving it.

4. **Lifetime ban admission**: The passenger states LATAM has banned him from flying and that LATAM controls ~80% of routes to his destination — establishing the disproportionate impact of the ban.

5. **Embassy engagement**: Confirms diplomatic channels were activated on 2024-07-06.

6. **High-probative-value**: As a constancia (official police statement), this recording carries evidentiary weight. The passenger's account is consistent with all other audio evidence recorded the previous day.
