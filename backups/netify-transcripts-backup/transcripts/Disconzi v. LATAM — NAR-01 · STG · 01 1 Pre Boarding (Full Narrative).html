The passenger's first interaction at SCL — a brief exchange with [[SPK-pilot-ruiz]] about the flight delay. The passenger asks why the flight is delayed and notes nobody notified passengers. The pilot confirms the flight remains within the operational schedule window. This legitimate question about the delay is the event that LATAM later characterizes as "disruptive" behavior — the trigger for the [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]]'s retaliation in STG-5. This recording establishes the passenger's conduct was a routine delay inquiry, not aggression or disruption.

Legal Significance

1. **Legitimate inquiry**: The passenger's question was about a flight delay affecting his connecting flight — a routine passenger inquiry, not disruption. This recording establishes the innocence of the act LATAM later used to justify removal.

2. **No aggression, no disruption**: The recording contains no raised voices, no threats, no refusal to comply. It is a brief, calm exchange about flight status.

3. **Captain Ruiz**: The pilot who later, according to [[SPK-joaquin-barraza-latam-security]] in STG-6, "decidió no embarcarlo en este vuelo" (decided not to board him on this flight). The captain's testimony about what he was told by the [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]] would be material.

4. **Causation chain**: This brief delay question → [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]] retaliation in STG-5 → false aggression accusation → forced removal → lifetime ban. The entire chain begins with a routine inquiry that LATAM weaponized.

5. **No notification to passengers**: "nadie nos avisa" — LATAM failed to notify passengers of the delay, a standard operational duty.
