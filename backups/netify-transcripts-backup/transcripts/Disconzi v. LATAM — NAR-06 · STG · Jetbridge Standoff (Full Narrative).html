Legal Significance

1. **Hearsay basis exposed**: The [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]] admits the accusation is "lo que me indican" — what others told her. Not personal observation. She was not a witness to any alleged aggression.

2. **Accusation escalation documented**: The claim shifts from "golpeo" (hit) to "no puede tocar" (can't touch) — a downgrade from criminal assault to physical contact, consistent with post-hoc rationalization.

3. **Captain Ruiz — chain of responsibility**: The removal is attributed to the captain. Counsel should determine: did Captain Ruiz make the decision independently, or was he acting on the [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]]'s false report? His testimony is material.

4. **Multiple passenger witnesses present**: Pasajeras 1-3, Pasajeros 4-5, Passagera 6 — independent third-party witnesses to the confrontation. Their testimony could corroborate that no aggression occurred. The transcript notes they were "questionando las narrativas" — questioning the shifting stories.

5. **[[SPK-joaquin-barraza-latam-security]]'s role**: Security chief who threatened "más problemas" if the passenger didn't comply, cited captain's authority, and facilitated the removal despite the hearsay basis of the accusation.

6. **Narrative shift in real time**: The passenger identifies the narrative changing during the interaction — from aggression to "entered gate alone." This is observed contemporaneously, not retrospectively.
