Summary

This is the originating incident — the boarding confrontation where a LATAM [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]] falsely accuses the passenger of aggression and calls "Ventas" (Sales) rather than security. The passenger repeatedly demands evidence of the alleged aggression, and the [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]] provides none. This accusation triggers the entire chain: PDI is summoned, the passenger is forcibly removed, a lifetime ban is imposed. The PDI subsequently reviewed CCTV and confirmed the accusation was false (STG-7). The [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]]'s call to "Ventas" — the same Sales department later used for document fabrication (STG-29) — establishes an institutional pattern of off-book operations.

**Violation:** CL-001 — False Accusation of Aggression (Calumnia) by LATAM [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]]

Legal Analysis

### The Accusation

The [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]] states at SEG 12.8: *"Por que fue agressivo y"* — the reason for removal was alleged aggression. At SEG 62.2, she restates: *"El motivo es que fue agresivo con la companera"* — specifying aggression against "the colleague."

The accusation is legally significant because:

1. It is the **sole stated reason** for the passenger's removal

2. PDI's CCTV review later **disproves** it entirely (STG-7 SEG 44: "no hay ninguna agresion como has visto en la camara," SEG 47: "lesiones no hay," SEG 140: PDI calls it a "lie")

3. The accusation is then **repeated in the fabricated carta de desembarque** (latam_STG_2 SEG 5) despite PDI's finding

4. It forms the basis for a **lifetime ban** that remains in effect

### The "Ventas" Call — Institutional Pattern

The [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]] calls "Ventas" (Sales) at SEG 28 (100.01s). This is not the security department. The call to Ventas establishes a pattern:

Ventas is consistently the operational channel for off-book actions — not Security, not Legal, not official channels.

### The Burden of Proof Inversion

The passenger repeatedly demands proof of the allegation (SEG 14.4, 23.5, 66.6, 76.1). The [[SPK-stewardess-accuser]] provides none. This inverts the legal burden: under CACH Art. 131 and LPDC Art. 23 bis, the carrier bears the burden of documenting the reasons for denied boarding. Here, the carrier makes an accusation, provides no proof, and removes the passenger — then refuses to document the reason for 9+ hours.
