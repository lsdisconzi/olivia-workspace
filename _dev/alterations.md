# New Terms and Legal Feedback — Comprehensive Review

## PART ONE: PROPOSED NEW TERMS

### 1. Terminology Refinements

| Current Term | Proposed Term | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Smoking gun" | **Contemporaneous admissions** | More legally precise; avoids colloquial overstatement |
| "False accusation" | **Baseless allegation** or **Disproven accusation** | "Baseless" emphasises the lack of evidence from the outset; "disproven" focuses on the PDI finding |
| "Coaching" | **Procedural coaching** or **Witness coaching** | More specific; aligns with the legal concept of interference with witness testimony |
| "Escalating justifications" | **Progressive pretext shifting** | Captures the pattern of adding new justifications as prior ones fail |
| "Deflection loop" | **Circular referral pattern** | Formal term for the ping-pong dynamic |
| "Documentation vacuum" | **Documentary denial** or **Systematic record withholding** | Emphasises the active (not passive) nature of the refusal |
| "Institutional ignorance" | **Institutional non-accountability** | More precise; it's not that staff are ignorant, but that the system has no mechanism to respond |
| "Retaliation admission" | **Explicit retaliatory intent** | More direct legal characterisation |
| "Criminal classification threat" | **Penal complaint threat** or **Criminal denuncia** | "Categoría 3" is LATAM's term; the legal concept is a criminal complaint (denuncia) |
| "Passa diario" | **Admission of systemic practice** | Translates the significance into legal terms |
| "Falsified document" | **Irregular document** or **Procedurally defective document** | "Falsified" implies forgery, which may require proof of intent to deceive; "irregular" is more defensible until all elements are established |
| "Motive-blind" | **No real-time verification requirement** | More neutral; describes the system accurately without implying malice |
| "Chilean officials force removal" | **DGAC-facilitated removal** | More specific about which officials were involved |
| "Luggage Department & Threats" | **Luggage Counter — Second Removal Threat** | More direct about what was documented |
| "Counter Humiliation" | **Counter Confrontation — Retaliation Admissions** | More specific about what was actually captured |

### 2. New Composite Terms

| New Term | Definition | Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Procedural Entrapment** | A system in which a passenger's reasonable resistance to an unfounded removal order is converted into the legal basis for the removal itself | Describes how the "desobediencia" classification was applied after the false accusation |
| **Documentary Asymmetry** | The imbalance in access to records: institutions have documents (carta de desembarque, internal emails), victims do not | Underpins the systemic denial of access to information |
| **Institutional Passivity Doctrine** | The position that a regulatory body (DGAC) cannot or will not investigate the underlying basis for an airline's removal decision | Captures the "atados de mano" admission |
| **Pretext Shifting** | The practice of substituting new justifications for an action when previous ones are disproven | Applies to the three (and later four) justifications for removal |
| **Retaliatory Escalation** | The pattern of increasing punitive measures (removal → ban → criminal complaint) in response to the victim's assertion of rights | Describes the escalation from removal to Categoría 3 classification |
| **Status-Based Institutional Depersonalisation** | The linguistic and procedural reduction of a passenger to a "normal citizen" — stripping them of the specific rights associated with passenger status | Describes the "ciudadano normal" language and its effect |
