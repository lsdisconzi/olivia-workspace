# Olivia Canonical Knowledge

## Purpose

This document combines the main Olivia narrative into one concise knowledge reference for agents and future documentation work.

## What Olivia is

Olivia is not a single-purpose application. It is a shared, intelligent workspace that any organisation can shape to its own world. It is a clean canvas already wired with the ability to think, learn, and build alongside the people who use it.

The central idea is simple: people closest to the work understand the problems best, while AI provides the technical execution.

## History and evolution

Olivia began as a legal-specific tool and quickly revealed a broader pattern: every industry holds operational knowledge inside its people, but very little of it becomes software because the people who hold it are not programmers and the tools offered to them force them to adapt to someone else’s idea of work.

That legal-specific logic was stripped away, leaving a general environment where domain expertise can meet AI and produce tools, workflows, and automations that fit real daily routines.

## Mission

The mission is to democratise the creation of AI-powered capability.

The core promises are:

1. Adapt to the organisation, not the other way around.
2. Turn knowledge into working software with domain experts collaborating directly with AI.
3. Enable learning while building.
4. Unite the organisation in one living ecosystem.
5. Grow with the people rather than being limited to predefined features.
6. Keep people at the centre while AI accelerates implementation.

## Core positioning

Olivia should be described as:

- an AI operating environment
- a workspace for building practical tools and workflows
- a platform where non-technical domain experts can collaborate with AI
- an extensible ecosystem that grows with the organisation

## Use cases

Olivia is relevant across sectors including hospitality, retail, legal, charity, healthcare, and operations. Typical examples include:

- building shift handbooks and service playbooks
- creating internal assistants for recurring tasks
- turning tacit knowledge into repeatable tools
- improving training and onboarding
- reducing friction between teams and departments
