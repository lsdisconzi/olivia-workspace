## What it is
Olivia is not a single-purpose app. It’s a **shared, intelligent workspace** that any team can shape to its own world — a clean canvas already wired with the ability to think, learn, and build alongside the people who use it. Technology has always asked people to learn software; Olivia asks software to learn people.

In practice: a kitchen team member with zero coding experience opens Olivia and says, *“This is how we prep the breakfast shift.”* Olivia asks the questions a good colleague would — where delays happen, what goes wrong, who is responsible. Minutes later, the team has built a custom AI tool for that shift, and every future shift benefits.

---

## How it works
**Conversation → Understanding → Creation → Knowledge → Organisation evolves**

- **Talk to Olivia** in plain language about your real daily routines.
- **Olivia asks clarifying questions**, learning your context and capturing the tacit knowledge that normally stays trapped in people’s heads.
- **Together you build tools, automations, and assistants** that fit exactly how your team works — prep checklists, handover notes, training aids, compliance steps, event run-sheets.
- **Because it’s one living workspace**, know‑how compounds across the whole organisation instead of walking out the door when someone leaves.

For a hospitality group, that means a barista’s opening routine can become a shared tool that trains new hires, while a shift-lead’s close-down checklist can evolve every service.

## History
Olivia began as a narrow legal tool. The real problem quickly revealed itself: every industry holds immense operational knowledge inside its people, yet almost none of it ever becomes software — because the people who hold it aren’t programmers, and the software they’re offered forces them to adapt to someone else’s idea of how work should be done.

The legal-specific logic was stripped away. What remained was a clean canvas — an environment where any domain’s expertise can meet AI and produce tools, workflows, and automations that fit real daily routines. That’s the AI Operating Environment.

We exist so that every business, charity, team, and individual worker can translate their operational expertise into practical, AI-assisted systems — without becoming software engineers.

## In Practice: Scottish Hospitality
Olivia’s hospitality branch is being brought directly to operators who already carry enormous tacit knowledge in their teams. The proposition is simple:

> “A chance to do good — turn your team’s know‑how into tools (no coding needed).”

Through hands‑on, no‑obligation demonstrations built around a venue’s real routines, operators can:
- Turn a busy Saturday service into a shared running order that adapts to live conditions.
- Codify a cocktail spec library and handover standards that any bartender can follow.
- Build a training path from casual café to fine dining, carrying the group’s identity across venues.

**Personalised examples from current outreach:**

- **Michele Paglioca (The Butterfly & The Pig, Glasgow):** A five‑venue mix of restaurant, live music, and nightclubs. Olivia captures the knowledge of best shift‑leads — Saturday service flow, DJ‑night staffing — and turns it into reusable tools for every site.

- **Luigi Aseni (Boteco do Brasil, Glasgow):** Brazilian restaurant and nightclub group. Olivia creates a shared workspace where cocktail specs, samba‑night plans, and event run‑sheets live and improve, across all four entities under the KAZZ holding.

- **Michael Berguson (Buck’s Bar & Thundercat, Glasgow):** Two‑venue group growing fast. Olivia provides consistency without bureaucracy — a “Thundercat way” for live‑sports service, a burger‑build standard at Buck’s — all built and refined by the teams themselves.

- **Dean Banks Group (St Andrews / East Coast):** Six venues from fine dining to Mediterranean. Olivia acts as a shared intelligence layer: head chefs contribute standards to a group‑wide sourcing note, a training path from Dune to Haar, and a guest‑experience playbook — while each venue keeps its own character.

- **Chris “Gaz” (Ronzio Broomhill, Glasgow):** A brand‑new 2025 brunch and speciality coffee venue. The rare advantage of building operational playbooks from day one — baristas and kitchen team describe their ideal service, and Olivia turns it into living tools that the whole team can shape.
