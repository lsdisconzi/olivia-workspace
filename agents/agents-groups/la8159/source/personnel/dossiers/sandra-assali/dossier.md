## 4. Connection to LA8159 fact pattern
Per `branch_documentation/ABEAR_CONTENT_REPORT.md` §"Sandra Assali — Victim → Advocate → Institutional Insider" (line 423): Assali presents the unusual trajectory of starting as a victim/advocate (ABRAPAVAA was founded as a victims' association) and over time consolidating institutional access — co-organizing events with LATAM operational leadership, meeting with federal Civil Aviation officials.
