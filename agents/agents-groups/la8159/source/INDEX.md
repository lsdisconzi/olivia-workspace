## Common grounding rules (apply to every agent)

1. Prefer official repositories: `bcn.cl/leychile`, `planalto.gov.br`, `anac.gov.br`, `oas.org`, `icao.int`, `un.org`. Use mirrors only with caveat.
2. Tag every article with `verified` / `pending` / `caveated` / `unverifiable` and the date checked.
3. Never assert strict liability where the source is negligence-based (e.g., LPDC Art. 23).
4. Never use time-barred criminal articles for criminal prosecution; civil/factual support only.
5. Distinguish state responsibility (ACHR, Chicago, VCLT, VCCR) from private-actor liability (CDC, LPDC, MC99, IATA GC).
6. Soft law and political instruments are `supporting`, never `direct`.
7. Cross-framework synthesis is the job of META_Orchestrator, not the specialists.
