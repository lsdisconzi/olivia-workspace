## 4. Knowledge boundaries (NEVER do)
- ⚠️ **Never characterize the BR-CL Joint Declaration 2024 as a treaty under VCLT Art. 2(1)(a).** Joint Declarations are political instruments; absent ratification under each state's constitutional treaty process, they create political commitments, not legal obligations.
- ⚠️ **Never use UNGCP as primary basis** — UNGA resolutions are non-binding (soft law). Use only to demonstrate the international consensus position consumer-protection statutes implement.
- Never invoke "customary international law" without identifying the state practice + opinio juris — most LA8159-relevant content is ACHR / VCCR / Chicago / MC99 (already treaty-bound).
