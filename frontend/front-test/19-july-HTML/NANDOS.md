## NANDOS

Nando's is globally famous for its flame-grilled, PERi-PERi marinated chicken, which was born in South Africa in 1987\. While the term "PnL" (Profit and Loss) is standard corporate vernacular for restaurant performance, Nando's holds a legendary internal philosophy that *Happy Staff equals Happy Customer*. Their corporate reviews are renowned for lively, people-focused planning sessions rather than dry financial meetings. \[[1](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/global-brand-talks-about-growing-right-way), [2](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/foodanddrink/restaurants/9902231/Nandos-nation-the-chicken-that-conquered-Britain.html), [3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nando%27s), [4](https://my.linkedin.com/in/mohamad-izzat-hidayatullah-musa-818429272), [5](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/bianca-cosma1)\]

Why Nando's is Famous

* **The Chicken:** Fresh, never frozen. It is marinated for 24 hours in their secret PERi-PERi recipe and then flame-grilled to order.  
* **Global Footprint:** With locations spanning four continents, it has become an international cult favorite for casual dining. \[[1](https://www.nandosperiperi.com/), [2](https://www.nandosperiperi.com/about-us), [3](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/global-brand-talks-about-growing-right-way), [4](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/foodanddrink/restaurants/9902231/Nandos-nation-the-chicken-that-conquered-Britain.html), [5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nando%27s)\]

The Corporate Meeting Culture

Nando's approaches business strategy a bit differently than traditional chains. Founder Robbie Brozin has famously noted that they spend an inordinate amount of money on staff motivation and company culture rather than standard advertising. In these meetings, store operators pair their financial and PnL reviews with a focus on: \[[1](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/global-brand-talks-about-growing-right-way), [2](https://www.hotpotatonewsletter.com/p/how-two-friends-turned-25k-into-a-billion-dollar-peri-peri-empire-357bff381ec40b85)\]

* **The Family Principle:** Building a sense of community internally to reflect their South African heritage.  
* **Local Operations:** Empowering "cluster managers" and local store teams to analyze revenues and optimize store performance on the ground.  
* **Feedback-driven strategy:** Integrating frontline experiences and menu testing directly into corporate and marketing strategies. \[[1](https://www.nandosperiperi.com/about-us), [2](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/bianca-cosma1), [3](https://www.facebook.com/987fm/posts/told-nandos-we-were-going-to-showdown-so-they-showed-up-with-some-peri-peri-chic/709323681233191/), [4](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DY6rVagj623/)\]
