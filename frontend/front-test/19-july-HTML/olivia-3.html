What it is — and what it isn't

Not software you buy off the shelf. An ecosystem.

Olivia isn't another chatbot, another AI assistant, or another workflow builder. It's a shared, intelligent workspace that any business can shape to its own world — a clean canvas, already wired with the ability to think, learn, and build alongside the people who use it.

Imagine

Maria opens Olivia. She has never written code.

She works in a restaurant and simply begins talking: "This is how we prepare breakfast." Olivia asks the questions a good colleague would — where do delays happen, what usually goes wrong, who is responsible, how do you know when everything is ready.

"This is how I do this job today. Here's what goes wrong. Here's what good looks like. How could we make a tool that helps?"

Five minutes later, Maria has built her first AI tool — and learned AI, without ever studying it.

History

It began as a series of questions.

The earliest conversations centred on a narrow legal tool. But the real problem was far larger: every industry holds immense operational knowledge inside its people, yet almost none of it ever becomes software — because the people who hold it aren't programmers, and the software they're offered forces them to adapt to someone else's idea of how their work should be done.

The turning point was recognising that organisations don't need another single-purpose application. They need an environment where their own expertise can meet AI — one that produces tools, workflows, and automations that fit their real daily routines. The legal-specific logic was stripped away. What remained was a clean canvas, already wired with the ability to think, learn, and build alongside the people who use it.

Mission

Democratise the creation of AI-powered capability.

We exist so that every business, charity, team, and individual worker can translate their operational expertise into practical, AI-assisted systems — without having to become software engineers.

  • 01Adapts to the organisation, not the other way around — learning from the people who know the work best.
  • 02Turns knowledge into working software, with domain experts collaborating directly with AI agents.
  • 03Enables learning while building — every interaction builds both capability and skill.
  • 04Unites the organisation in one living ecosystem, turning isolated efforts into shared intelligence.
  • 05Grows with the people who use it — never limited to predefined features.
  • 06Keeps people at the centre — AI accelerates implementation, people provide context, judgment, and priorities.
