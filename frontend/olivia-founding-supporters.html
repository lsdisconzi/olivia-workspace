Worth knowing

One honest paragraph.

Olivia is operated by Awareness‑AI, a private company. Root Supporter and Grove Partner contributions are gifts to that company — not charitable donations, and not tax‑deductible. They carry no promise of return. The Investor Track is different: it's a genuine investment conversation, but nothing is agreed or processed on this page — terms are set out formally in writing, individually, once we've spoken, in a way that fits wherever you're based. Nothing on this page is financial advice, and nothing here is an offer to sell securities.
Questions

A few things people ask.

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

No — Awareness‑AI is a private company, not a registered charity. Root and Grove contributions are gifts, plain and simple.

Can I get equity for a smaller amount?

Not through Root or Grove — those are gift tiers by design. If equity's what you're after, that's the Investor Track.

What happens after I submit the Investor Track form?

Someone gets in touch directly — no obligation either way. If it's a fit, terms get drawn up properly from there.

I'm not in the UK — can I still take part?

Yes. Root and Grove are open globally. Investor Track conversations are handled per jurisdiction, since the paperwork differs by country.
